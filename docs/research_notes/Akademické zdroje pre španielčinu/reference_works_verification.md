# Verification of Spanish reference works and corpora (second pass)

Research note (2026-11-09). Method: 17 tool calls. All direct fetches of rae.es, dle.rae.es and routledge.com failed (HTTP 403 or wrong page returned). Facts therefore come from web-search result summaries that quote catalogue records, bookseller listings, press and RAE page titles/snippets. No primary page was read in full, so every ISBN below is "catalogue-reported", not title-page-checked. Items not found are in Gaps. Complements (does not repeat) `grammars_dictionaries_corpora.md`.

## 1. Grammars

### Takeaway
Verified at catalogue level: Bosque & Demonte 1999 (3 vols, Espasa), Butt/Benjamin/Moreira Rodríguez 6th ed. (Routledge 2018), RAE Manual (2010), Gramática básica (2011), Aragonés & Palencia Gramática de uso (SM), Matte Bon (Edelsa, 2 tomos). Not found: Alarcos 1994, Kattán-Ibarra & Pountain, Castro's Uso de la gramática española. The NGLE is Pan-Hispanic by design; the others are Spain-centred or general unless noted.

### Inferences
- Mexican/American coverage: NGLE, Manual, Básica (ASALE co-authored, 22/23 academies) are the only grammars in the list that treat American usage systematically; the NGLE itself says it is not a dialectology treatise. Butt/Benjamin, Bosque/Demonte, Aragonés/Palencia, Matte Bon are Spain-based (their authors' base; not verified from these records) and must be cross-checked for Mexico (ustedes, pretérito vs compuesto, leísmo) before a tip claims a Mexican norm.
- How a content team uses them: NGLE/Manual as primary citation by § number; Aragonés/Palencia and Matte Bon for learner-level explanations and example sentences by CEFR level; Butt/Benjamin for English-language cross-check of a rule; Bosque/Demonte only for theoretical detail (not for A1–B2 tips).
- Access: all above are paid print/e-book; Manual and Básica have free online versions on rae.es (seen only as page titles); NGLE 2nd ed. online status unconfirmed.

## 2. Dictionaries

### Takeaway
DLE is free online; the latest update seen is 23.8.1 (Dec 2025), with the 24th edition announced for Nov 2026. DPD 2nd ed. appeared in 2025 (publication Feb vs presentation June). Moliner's 3rd ed. (Gredos 2007) is the latest found. Seco/Andrés/Ramos 2nd ed. 2011 could not be confirmed. REDES verified only by title/publisher.

### Cited Findings
- DLE updates: 23.8 (Dec 2024) = 4,074 novelties (609 new words, 1,304 modifications), per El Imparcial; 23.8.1 presented 15 Dec 2025 with ~330 novelties, described as a preview of the 24th edition planned for Nov 2026 — [El Imparcial](https://www.elimparcial.com/mundo/2024/12/11/desde-espoiler-hasta-dana-la-rae-se-actualiza-con-mas-de-4000-novedades-en-el-diccionario/), [Metro PR](https://www.metro.pr/estilo-vida/2025/12/15/rae-anade-loguearse-microteatro-milenial-y-turismofobia-en-nueva-actualizacion-del-diccionario/), [El Diario EC](https://www.eldiario.ec/centro/crudivorismo-loguearse-y-turismofobia-las-nuevas-palabras-del-diccionario-de-la-rae-20251215/). ### Inferences
- Cite DLE with version (e.g. "DLE 23.8, consultado ..."); since 23.8.1 is not the 23.8 of 2024, always note the access date; the 24th ed. is announced for Nov 2026, so entries cited now may change.
- Mexican coverage: DLE marks "Méx."; DPD is pan-Hispanic; Moliner, Seco, REDES are Spain-based and not markers of Mexican norm.
- Use: DLE for existence/senses/gender; DPD for norm doubts; REDES for collocations (verb+noun) when writing example sentences; Moliner/DEA for senses and usage examples.

## 3. Corpora and frequency

### Takeaway
CORPES XXI is free on rae.es; size has grown (v1.3 July 2025 ~438M forms; a v1.5 dated 29 Jun 2026 ~455M, per search summary) and is country-balanced by design (target 70% America / 30% Spain). CEMC and CSCM exist but have no found live query interface; CSCM volumes are free PDFs.

### Cited Findings
- CORPES XXI: RAE reference corpus; ~25M forms per year of the 21st century from all Spanish-speaking countries, planned 30% Spain / 70% America; v1.5 "casi 455 millones de formas" and ~420,000 documents, dated 29 Jun 2026 in a search summary; v1.3 (Jul 2025) ~438M forms, >405,000 documents; headline "supera los 455 millones de formas"; free in the RAE web "banco de datos" at rae.es/banco-de-datos/corpes-xxi (no fee seen; registration not confirmed) — [RAE CORPES page](https://www.rae.es/banco-de-datos/corpes-xxi), [RAE news: supera los 455 millones](https://www.rae.es/noticia/el-corpes-supera-los-455-millones-de-formas), [RAE 2025 CORPES written manual PDF](https://www.rae.es/sites/default/files/2026-01/2025_Manual_CORPES_Escrito.pdf), [text-selection parameters](https://www.rae.es/banco-de-datos/corpes-xxi/parametros-de-seleccion-de-textos) (all seen as search snippets; fetch gave 403; version numbers differ across snippets)
- CEMC: Corpus del Español Mexicano Contemporáneo (El Colegio de México, Luis Fernando Lara), ~2 million words, period 1921–1974; address given as cemc.colmex.mx in an older article; a second CEMC (1975–2018) reported as finished in a 2019 DEM presentation; base of the Diccionario del español de México — [Signos Lingüísticos article](https://signoslinguisticos.izt.uam.mx/index.php/SL/article/download/406/353), [DEM presentation PDF](https://organoscolegiados.anuies.mx/consejonacional/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2019/10/11.1.-Presentación-DEM-COLMEX.pdf)
- CSCM: Corpus sociolingüístico de la Ciudad de México (coord. Pedro Martín Butragueño & Yolanda Lastra), the Mexico City module of PRESEEA, 108 interviews recorded from 1997; volumes by education level (superior 2011; media; baja 2015), with free PDFs at El Colegio de México (repositorio.colmex.mx; lef.colmex.mx) and CD-ROM in print — [Colmex repository (vol. baja)](https://repositorio.colmex.mx/downloads/hq37vr51k?locale=en), [LEF PDF media](https://lef.colmex.mx/pdf/CSCMmedio.pdf), [LEF PDF baja](https://lef.colmex.mx/pdf/CSCMbajo.pdf)
- Corpus del Español (Davies): Web/Dialects 2B words, NOW 7.3B — [corpusdelespanol.org](https://www.corpusdelespanol.org/) (from previous note)

### Inferences
- Country filter: CORPES XXI supports country selection (per RAE design statements; the filter UI itself was not seen). For "is this natural in Mexico" check México vs España counts in CORPES; CSCM transcripts only as spoken-register evidence.
- Frequency lists: use Davies & Davies (previous note) for rank, CORPES for country distribution.

### Gaps
- CREA/CORDE sizes and status; CORPES frequency list URL; PRESEEA other Mexican cities (Guadalajara, Monterrey) data; CEDEL2 (Lozano); SPLLOC; Sketch Engine esTenTen sizes and free access; Corpus del Español free-access limits and country filter: none found in this pass. UNVERIFIED.
- Whether CORPES XXI requires registration (not stated in any snippet).
- CEMC live URL and access terms.

## 4. Normative services

### Takeaway
RAE "Consultas lingüísticas" is verified as a free service, with a search of answered queries plus a form. Fundéu and AML Consultas only partially verified.

### Cited Findings
- RAE Consultas lingüísticas: users can search already-answered consultations (edited and anonymized); if no answer, they submit a form and RAE linguists reply by e-mail — [RAE Consultas info](https://www.rae.es/leia/consultas/info), [RAE Consultas](https://www.rae.es/leia/consultas) (search snippets; 403 on direct fetch). Example entry: [México o Méjico](https://www.rae.es/leia/consultas/se-escribe-mexico-o-mejico)
- AML Comisión de Consultas answers questions on language use put to the Academia Mexicana de la Lengua by authorities or individuals; queries sent through the AML website — [UNAM thesis](https://ru.dgb.unam.mx/bitstream/20.500.14330/TES01000852455/3/0852455.pdf) (secondary source; AML site not opened)
- Fundéu: described as an institution advised by the RAE promoting correct use of Spanish in the media; a 2024–25 thesis lists a FundéuRAE query service — [Barcelona TFG](https://diposit.ub.edu/dspace/bitstream/2445/222481/1/TFG_Honorato_Mazza_Bel%c3%a9n.pdf) (secondary only)

### Inferences
- Cite a specific consultation entry only after reading it; for Mexican norm, AML/DEM beat Spain-based services.

### Gaps
- Fundéu's own site and rename to FundéuRAE not verified; AML "Español de México" pages not opened; "Español al día" not located (the RAE service seen is named "Consultas lingüísticas", on /leia/).
