# Authoritative reference works for Spanish: grammars, dictionaries, frequency resources, corpora, usage services

Research note (2026-11-09). Method: ~13 web searches/fetches. rae.es pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so several RAE facts rest on press coverage or on the researcher's background knowledge. Background-knowledge items are labelled "UNVERIFIED" and appear only under Inferences/Gaps, never under Cited Findings.

## 1. Academic and descriptive grammars (NGLE, Bosque & Demonte, Butt & Benjamin, others)

### Takeaway
The standard normative-descriptive reference is the RAE & ASALE Nueva gramática de la lengua española (NGLE). Its second, revised and expanded edition was presented on 13 Nov 2024 in Quito, and it is a pan-Hispanic work that pays attention to variation. Descriptive alternatives (Bosque & Demonte 1999, Butt & Benjamin) are UNVERIFIED in this research and need a manual check of their citation data.

### Cited Findings
- NGLE 2nd edition: presented 13 Nov 2024 at the close of the XVII ASALE Congress in Quito; "revised and expanded", improved descriptions, added complementary bibliography per chapter, longer list of cited texts; it "is still not a treatise of dialectology but pays attention to variation"; the paper edition (Espasa) was announced for "the coming months" — [Excélsior](https://www.excelsior.com.mx/expresiones/novedades-asale-ngle-dpd/1684253)
- ASALE gathers the academies of 23 Spanish-speaking countries, so ASALE-co-authored works (NGLE, DPD, Diccionario de americanismos, Ortografía) are pan-Hispanic by design — [Excélsior](https://www.excelsior.com.mx/expresiones/novedades-asale-ngle-dpd/1684253)
- Official RAE/ASALE press note on the presentation of novelties in the RAE and ASALE works (PDF, 2023): [ASALE NdP](https://wwwpro.asale.org/sites/default/files/2023-03/NdP_RAE_Presentaci%C3%B3n%20de%20novedades%20en%20las%20obras%20de%20la%20RAE%20y%20de%20la%20ASALE.pdf) (title only checked from search listing; content not read)

### Inferences
- Cite NGLE by edition (2009 first ed.; 2024 second ed. online/paper) and by paragraph number (§) in tips, because the 2nd edition changed some descriptions. Which edition's numbering a tip uses should be recorded in the citation.
- UNVERIFIED (background knowledge): NGLE 2009, 2 vols + Fonética y fonología vol. (2011), Espasa; Manual de la NGLE (2010) and Gramática básica de la lengua española (2011) are the condensed versions; Bosque & Demonte (dirs.), Gramática descriptiva de la lengua española, 3 vols., Espasa-Calpe 1999 (Spain-centred, more theoretical); Butt & Benjamin, A New Reference Grammar of Modern Spanish, Routledge (5th ed. 2011 per memory; a later edition may exist), English-language, comparative with Spain-focused norms; Alarcos Llorach, Gramática de la lengua española, Espasa 1994 (structuralist); Kattán-Ibarra & Pountain, Modern Spanish Grammar, Routledge (reference grammar with workbook, English-language). Use NGLE as the primary arbiter; use Butt & Benjamin only as an English-language cross-check.

### Gaps
- Free-online status of NGLE 2nd edition and the exact rae.es URL could not be confirmed (rae.es returned 403; press only says the online version is "coming").
- ISBNs, publisher and edition data for Bosque & Demonte, Butt & Benjamin, Alarcos, Kattán-Ibarra not verified in this pass.

## 2. Learner grammars for ELE (Difusión, SM, Edelsa, etc.)

### Takeaway
The Difusión Gramática básica del estudiante de español (GBEE) is verified, with several editions including a 2022 "versión panhispánica", which is the one relevant for Mexican content. Other ELE grammars are listed from memory only.

### Cited Findings
- Gramática básica del estudiante de español, Difusión: revised edition 2011, ISBN 978-84-8443-726-0 (312 pp. per one catalogue; one retailer lists 7 Sept 2011); earlier 2010 printing ISBN 9788484432258; description as self-study pedagogical grammar for levels A1–B1 of the CEFR — [Casadellibro/booklist results](https://www.casadellibro.com/libro-gramatica-basica-del-estudiante-de-espanol/9780131598706/5771398), [library record](https://nantilus.univ-nantes.fr/vufind/Record/PPN158648919)
- "Gramática básica del estudiante de español. Versión panhispánica", ISBN 978-84-18907-37-1 (2022 per ISBN registry) — [ISBN registry](https://www.isbnlab.com/book/9788418907371/gramatica-basica-del-estudiante-de-espanol-version-panhispanica)
- North American edition (Pearson/Prentice Hall, 2007), ISBN 9780131598706 — [booklist](https://www.biggerbooks.com/gram225tica-b225sica-del-estudiante-de/bk/9780131598706)
- A review/description of the GBEE as a textbook exists in MarcoELE — [MarcoELE PDF](https://marcoele.com/descargas/13/llopis-gbe.pdf) (not read in full)

### Inferences
- The GBEE targets A1–B1, not B2, so B2 tips need NGLE/Manual or Gramática de uso B1–B2. Prefer the panhispánica version for Mexican claims; the original is Spain-oriented UNVERIFIED.
- UNVERIFIED (background knowledge, check before citing): Gramática de uso del español A1–A2 / B1–B2 / C1–C2 (Aragonés & Palencia, SM); Uso de la gramática española, elemental / intermedio / avanzado (Francisca Castro, Edelsa); Gramática comunicativa del español (Matte Bon, Edelsa, 2 vols.); Gramática práctica del español actual (Aragonés/Palencia? or Alonso/Prieto? — attribution not verified). Do not cite these without checking title page data.

### Gaps
- No verification of SM, Edelsa, Matte Bon titles, editions or levels; no data on which have Latin American editions.

## 3. Dictionaries (DLE, DPD, americanismos, DEM, mexicanismos, learner, bilingual SK/CZ)

### Takeaway
DPD 2nd edition (2025) and the DEM (online since 2012, 2nd print edition) are verified and are the key normative and Mexico-specific references. The Diccionario de americanismos (ASALE 2010) is verified and free online via rae.es. Moliner, Seco, Clave, REDES, Salamanca are UNVERIFIED here. For Slovak, Lingea is the verified modern bilingual dictionary.

### Cited Findings
- DPD, 2nd ed.: more than 7,000 entries, 370 new entries (362 non-thematic, mostly neologisms/loanwords, plus 8 thematic such as gerundio, prefijos), amendments to almost half of non-thematic entries, incorporates NGLE's normative innovations; first edition 2005; electronic format on rae.es; press dates differ (Feb 2025 vs. 19 June 2025 presentation) — [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/cultura/20250213/rae-publica-segunda-edicion-diccionario-panhispanico-dudas_269979.html), [Excélsior](https://www.excelsior.com.mx/expresiones/novedades-asale-ngle-dpd/1684253). Paper edition (Taurus) announced.
- Diccionario de americanismos (RAE & ASALE, Santillana 2010, dir. Humberto López Morales): 70,000 entries/locutions, 120,000 senses; online version on rae.es — [RAE DAMER](https://www.rae.es/recursos/diccionarios/damer)
- Diccionario del español de México (DEM), El Colegio de México: current online version since Aug 2012 at dem.colmex.mx; 2nd edition ~90,000 senses with example sentences; print edition in 2 vols. (2010); usage-based, not only mexicanismos; antecedents Diccionario básico del español de México (1986), Diccionario del español usual en México (1996) — [Excélsior summary & library records via search](https://www.excelsior.com.mx/expresiones/aceptan-guey-y-chafa-en-el-diccionario-del-espanol-de-mexico/1369557), [UAM catalogue vol. 1](https://biblioteca.izt.uam.mx/vufind/Record/DIGCOLMX0111), [Andamios article](https://andamios.uacm.edu.mx/index.php/andamios/article/view/200/epub)
- Academia Mexicana de la Lengua, Diccionario de mexicanismos: 1st ed. 2010 (Siglo XXI, dir. Concepción Company, 648 pp. in one record); a newer edition "Propios y compartidos" published late 2022; AML website also hosts a short Diccionario breve de mexicanismos (Gómez de Silva), undated — [Yucatán.com.mx](https://www.yucatan.com.mx/imagen/2023/01/29/nuevo-diccionario-expone-la-riqueza-de-mexicanismos-378953.html), [LanguageHat](https://languagehat.com/mexican-and-geographical-dictionaries)
- Spanish–Slovak: Lingea "Veľký španielsko-slovenský slovensko-španielsky slovník" is cited as a source in a Slovak academic article; older Škultéty & Šulhan, Španielsko-slovenský a slovensko-španielsky slovník (SPN Bratislava, 1988); Slovart only had a 2006 trilingual illustrated dictionary — [Lingea catalogue](https://www.lingea.sk/files/katalóg všeobecný-jazyky.pdf), [Studia Romanistica 2017 review](https://dokumenty.osu.cz/ff/journals/studiaromanistica/17-1/SR_1_2017_12_Ulasin.pdf), [library record](https://digilibs.fchpt.stuba.sk/Record/stu.stu31346/Description), [SNK record](https://katalog.snk.sk/vufind/Record/654520/Description)
- Spanish–Czech: "Španělsko-český, česko-španělský šikovný slovník… nejen do školy" (2023), ISBN 9788075089182 — [listing](https://czechmovie.com/products/9788075089182) (a school-level dictionary, low authority)

### Inferences
- Verification routine for content: DLE (is it a word, senses, marks like "Méx.") -> DPD (norm doubts) -> DEM (Mexican usage, example sentences) -> Diccionario de americanismos (distribution of americanisms) -> AML mexicanismos (Mexican-only items). Bilingual SK dictionaries only help for Slovak glosses; they are not normative.
- UNVERIFIED (background knowledge): DLE 23rd ed. (2014, updated online yearly, free at dle.rae.es); María Moliner, Diccionario de uso del español (Gredos, 3rd ed. 2007); Seco/Andrés/Ramos, Diccionario del español actual (Aguilar, 1999; 2nd ed. 2011 as Diccionario del español actual? unverified); Clave (SM); REDES (RAE/Bosque, SM 2004, combinatory dictionary), Diccionario combinatorio práctico (Bosque); Diccionario Salamanca de la lengua española (Santillana 1996); Diccionario del estudiante (RAE/Santillana). The existence of free online Spanish-Czech academic dictionaries was not checked.

### Gaps
- Whether DEM online is still free and without limits; DPD 2nd edition's exact online URL and release date (press conflicts); DLE edition status (23.x/24th) — rae.es blocked.
- Online Spanish–Slovak dictionaries (e.g. Lingea online, slovnik.sk) not checked.

## 4. Frequency resources

### Takeaway
Davies & Davies, A Frequency Dictionary of Spanish, 2nd ed. (Routledge, 2018) is verified and is the standard frequency list (5,000 words by memory, UNVERIFIED), but it is based on Davies's own multi-register corpus rather than Mexico alone. CORPES XXI lists and the PCIC "Nociones específicas" inventory were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Davies, Mark & Kathy Hayward Davies, A Frequency Dictionary of Spanish: Core Vocabulary for Learners, 2nd ed., Routledge Frequency Dictionaries, 2018 (some sellers: Dec 2017); paperback ISBN 978-1-138-68654-0, hardback 978-1-138-68652-6, eBook 978-1-134-87453-8, CD-ROM 978-1-138-68656-4; hardback 350 pp. — [Routledge](https://www.routledge.com/A-Frequency-Dictionary-of-Spanish-Core-Vocabulary-for-Learners/Davies-Davies/p/book/9781138686540), [CiNii record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB25891681?l=en)
- Corpus del Español (Mark Davies) offers Genre/Historical (100M words), Web/Dialects (2B words, 2016), NOW (7.3B words, 2012–2019) and Google Books n-grams (45B words) — [corpusdelespanol.org](https://www.corpusdelespanol.org/)

### Inferences
- For A1–B2 level assignment, combine rank bands from Davies (e.g. top ~1,500 -> A1/A2 candidates) with the PCIC inventories and the in-app topic; frequency alone is a poor guide for topic vocabulary (e.g. food, body). Treat the result as a heuristic and document it.
- UNVERIFIED: free word-frequency lists from CREA ("Listado de frecuencias", 5,000 forms) and CORPES XXI frequency lists exist on rae.es; PCIC "Nociones específicas" are free on cvc.cervantes.es; no Spanish equivalent of English Vocabulary Profile is known to me.

### Gaps
- Not verified: CORPES/CREA frequency list URLs; PCIC inventory URLs; whether the Davies dictionary is variety-weighted toward Spain vs. Latin America.

## 5. Corpora (CREA, CORDE, CORPES XXI, Davies, CEMC, CSCM, PRESEEA, CAES, CEDEL2, SPLLOC)

### Takeaway
For checking whether a sentence is natural in Mexico: CORPES XXI (country filter) and Davies' Web/Dialects (country tag) are the practical tools; the CAES learner corpus (Instituto Cervantes) is verified and is the only learner corpus confirmed here. Other corpora are not verified.

### Cited Findings
- CAES (Corpus de Aprendices de Español): written texts by learners at A1–C1 with six L1s (Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, French, English, Portuguese, Russian); commissioned by the Instituto Cervantes from the Universidade de Santiago de Compostela; first public release 2014, v1.3 in April 2020 (one dataset card mentions v2.1); query tool reported at galvan.usc.es/caes — [USC chapter](https://gramatica.usc.es/~grojo/Publicaciones/CAES_y_sus_aplicaciones_final), [Benjamins](https://benjamins.com/catalog/scl.78.03roj), [HF dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmvasque/caes)
- Corpus del Español (Web/Dialects 2B words; NOW 7.3B words) as above — [corpusdelespanol.org](https://www.corpusdelespanol.org/)

### Inferences
- UNVERIFIED (background knowledge): CORPES XXI (RAE, texts from 2001, ~300M forms ... exact size unverified, searchable by country incl. México, free with registration-free access); CREA/CORDE (older RAE corpora, CREA frozen ~2004, being superseded); CEMC and CSCM (El Colegio de México; spoken Mexican Spanish); PRESEEA; CEDEL2 (Univ. Granada, L2 learners, free); SPLLOC (Southampton, learner Spanish). The Davies site's free tier has daily query limits (not confirmed here).
- Method for the content team: search an exact phrase in CORPES XXI with country = México and compare against Spain; absence is only weak evidence for unnaturalness.

### Gaps
- The CORPES XXI page and search results were unreachable (rae.es 403; the web search returned no hits). No verified URL, size or access terms for CORPES XXI, CREA, CORDE, CEMC, CSCM, PRESEEA, CEDEL2, SPLLOC.
- Free-tier limits of corpusdelespanol.org not confirmed.

## 6. Normative and usage advice services (Fundéu RAE, Español al día, AML consultas)

### Takeaway
Not researched with verified sources in this pass; the DPD (Section 3) is the verified normative anchor.

### Cited Findings
- The DPD 2nd edition's thematic entries and normative changes (e.g. accepted plurals másteres/másters) are the current RAE/ASALE normative position — [Excélsior](https://www.excelsior.com.mx/expresiones/novedades-asale-ngle-dpd/1684253)

### Inferences
- UNVERIFIED: Fundéu RAE (fundeu.es, advice on journalism/language), RAE "Español al día" consultation service, and AML consultas (academia.org.mx) all provide free doubt answers; they are secondary to DPD and should be cited only for the specific consultation entry.

### Gaps
- No verification of URLs, scope or response policy for Fundéu RAE, Español al día, AML consultas.
- Not found anywhere: Mexican/American-specific coverage details for leísmo, ustedes vs. vosotros and pretérito vs. perfecto compuesto in each work; these should be read in NGLE directly (the NGLE does record variation but is "not a dialectology treatise").
